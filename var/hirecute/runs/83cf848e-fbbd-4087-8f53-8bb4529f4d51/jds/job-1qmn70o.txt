Learn more about our flexible approach to where we work. Overview Instacart Ads is one of the fastest-growing and most strategically important parts of the Instacart business. Our Ads organization brings together product, engineering, data science, design, sales, and strategy to build a world-class advertising ecosystem — one that serves brands, retailers, and agencies looking to reach millions of shoppers at the point of purchase. The Advertiser & Partner Experience (APEX) team sits at the heart of this ecosystem. We own the flows and tools that advertisers use to create, manage, optimize, and understand the value they derive from campaigns and promotions across Instacart Ads. Our scope spans Ads Manager and its APIs, advertiser-facing reporting and insights, retailer advertising products, and Carrot Ads — a growing suite of tools that enables retailers to monetize their own e-commerce sites using Instacart's ad tech, brand partnerships, and expertise. We're looking for a Senior Product Manager to own a critical portion of the Advertiser Experience — specifically, the tools and platform capabilities that help advertisers understand the value they're getting from Instacart Ads, trust those results, and act on them. This PM will also own the core platform infrastructure that underpins all aspects of campaign creation, management, and reporting, including API, product library, user management, change history, billing, and notifications. This role is an opportunity to own a high-impact, highly visible product surface that directly shapes how advertisers engage with and derive value from one of the industry's most exciting retail media platforms. Over time, as you demonstrate mastery of your scope, you'll have the opportunity to take on increasing strategic ownership and step into a senior PM lead role for the Advertiser Experience team as a whole.
