At Render, we’re building the modern cloud platform for developers creating AI-native, full-stack, multi-service applications. Our mission is to eliminate the tradeoff between the power of hyperscalers and the simplicity of developer-friendly platforms—so teams can ship fast, scale reliably, and focus on their product, not infrastructure.

Unlike complex hyperscalers or ephemeral edge/serverless solutions, Render offers a developer-first experience with persistent compute, dynamic autoscaling, built-in orchestration, and observability, allowing teams to launch, scale, and manage real-world applications without writing infrastructure code or managing servers. Whether you're building LLM-powered applications, scalable SaaS products, or async processing pipelines, Render empowers teams to move fast and scale confidently from MVP to millions of users.

Our platform is trusted by over 7 million developers worldwide and continues to grow rapidly. In February 2026, we raised an additional $100M in Series C financing, bringing our total funding to $260M, to accelerate our vision of making cloud infrastructure both powerful and intuitive—designed for the speed of modern AI development.

We’re a diverse and talented team that values craft, velocity, and user experience. If you’re excited to help shape the future of the intelligent cloud and empower developers everywhere, we’d love to hear from you.

ABOUT THE ROLE

As a Senior Engineering Manager for Product Engineers, you will lead a group of talented builders creating the core developer experience of the Render platform. This role blends strong technical leadership with product thinking: you’ll partner closely with product managers and designers to translate user needs into high-quality, scalable systems that developers rely on every day. You’ll be responsible for guiding technical direction, developing engineers, building the team, and ensuring the team ships meaningful improvements quickly while maintaining the high standards that define Render’s platform. The ideal candidate brings a strong engineering background, a deep sense of ownership, and a passion for building products that developers love.




WHAT YOU'LL DO

 - Lead and grow a team of product engineers, supporting their development through mentorship, feedback, and clear technical guidance.

 - Hire out your team and build it into multiple teams.

 - Drive execution on key product initiatives from ideation through launch, balancing speed with high standards of reliability and quality.

 - Partner closely with Product and Design teams to translate customer needs into thoughtful technical solutions and product roadmaps.

 - Engage deeply in technical discussions, guiding system design decisions and helping the team navigate architectural tradeoffs.

 - Foster a culture of ownership, accountability, and continuous improvement across engineering practices and team processes.

 - Help the team break down complex problems into incremental milestones that deliver value quickly while supporting long-term platform evolution.

 - Ensure teams maintain a strong understanding of the developer experience and customer impact of the features they build.

 - Stakeholder Collaboration: Partner closely with product management, customers, and internal teams to understand needs, align priorities, and ensure delivery of impactful features and solutions.


WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

 - 8+ years as an Engineer shipping products and/or features that have downstream impact on the business and product ecosystem

 - 4+ years of experience managing people and developing engineers, including hiring, mentorship, and performance management.

 - Strong technical background with the ability to engage deeply in system design, architecture, and engineering tradeoffs.

 - Product-oriented mindset with experience translating business goals and user needs into engineering roadmaps and shipped features.

 - A track record of successful collaboration with product, marketing, and customer support teams to drive adoption and retention.

 - Ability to balance urgency with thoughtful execution, helping teams ship quickly while maintaining high quality. Able to turn ambiguous problems into achievable goals.

 - Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively across engineering, product, and design.

 - A strong sense of ownership and accountability for both team outcomes and long-term system health.

 - Experience improving engineering velocity through tooling, processes, and team practices.


NICE-TO-HAVES:

 - Experience building products for developer platforms, cloud infrastructure, or DevTools.

 - Background working on highly scalable distributed systems or infrastructure platforms.

 - Experience building products used directly by developers or technical users.
