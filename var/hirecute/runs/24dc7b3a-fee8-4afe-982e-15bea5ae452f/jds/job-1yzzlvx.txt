About the role: This is a defining opportunity for a Senior Product Manager to own how every Samsara device comes to life. Samsara ships hundreds of thousands of devices a year to fleets, construction sites, and depots worldwide. A single customer rollout can exceed 50,000 devices , where every minute saved per install compounds into weeks of deployment time. Each device has a five-minute window where it goes from a box in someone's hands to a live sensor feeding a customer's operation. You will own those five minutes and the multi-year vision for what they become. The install experience is critical to the compounding value of every Samsara product. A camera aimed correctly on day one trains better safety models. A gateway reporting cleanly makes every downstream workflow trustworthy. You will own the software install experience end to end, with a dedicated engineering and design team, and you will own one number: install correctness , the share of installs completed right on the first attempt. Correctness today swings by several points depending on whether the installer uses the Samsara Fleet App, and closing that gap for every install is the first prize. Your target is an install under five minutes of active app time that confirms its own health before the installer walks away. You are building for two people who each get the same five minutes. A contracted technician on their twentieth install of the day wants speed. A customer's shop mechanic on their first wants guidance that assumes nothing. Both are standing in a truck bay, holding a phone, sometimes without signal.
